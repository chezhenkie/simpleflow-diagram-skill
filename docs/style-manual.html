Flow diagram style manual

Specsheet for hand-written, single-file flow diagrams with a small toolbar for mode, theme and print. One vertical main path, optional side blocks, one theme for the page, no external dependencies. The template is copied, not rebuilt; the written part stays small because the grid, the type scale, the themes and the budgets are fixed.

11. File contract

  • One .html file per topic, <topic>-flow.html, copied from template.html.
  • UTF-8, printable ASCII only. No typographic quotes, dashes, arrows or bullets.
  • Inline scripts limited to the toolbar and its initial mode. No external CSS, fonts, images or icon sets. Must open from file://.
  • Light and dark from the toolbar switch, initial state from prefers-color-scheme; two menus pick a theme per mode; print is a separate toggle.
  • One <main> wrapper, max width 1080 px, centered; one <h1>, one .sub, one or more <section> blocks, then an empty <footer> that draws the closing rule. Footer text is optional. The SVG scales with the page, no fixed width or height attributes.

33. Theme

A theme is six values: bg page, card card and tag fill, ink page text and card titles, muted descriptions, captions, connectors, footer and toolbar ink, line card and tag borders and toolbar lines, accent pill outline, focus border and action arrow.

Nine themes ship in the template: five dark and four light. Dark: github, harbor, solarized, oledblack, ayu. Light: vercel, tokyonight, cursor, orng. Light opens on vercel, dark on github.

Four mark pairs keep a small shortfall under deuteranopia and are accepted deviations: vercel accent on page 2.91:1, tokyonight accent on card 2.97:1, tokyonight checked label 4.31:1 and orng checked label 4.00:1. The focus card still reads by 2 px width, the line marks clear 3:1 and the labels are text. Do not retune these tokens without an explicit request.

  • The agent does not pick, mix or author colors. The theme script in template.html writes the six values as CSS custom properties.
  • A diagram carries no colors of its own: no class on a section, no per-section token block, no inline fill or stroke with a color value.
  • The pill is transparent with a 2 px accent outline and takes the page ink for its text, in both modes. No theme fills the title box.
  • One theme per page; two diagrams use the same tokens and contrast comes from layout and copy.
  • Print is a separate state with fixed tokens: white page, black ink, #333333 muted, #9a9a9a line, black accent, and the same transparent pill with a 2 px black outline and black text.

44. Type scale

Class sizes: h1 22/400, .sub 13, h2 16/600, .note 13, .t 14.5/600, .d 12.5, .cap 11.5, .side 11.5, footer 12. Body stack "Segoe UI", system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif, 15px/1.45. The budgets leave 10 percent headroom for wider fallback fonts; a size change invalidates them.

66. Card anatomy

A card is <rect class="card"> or <rect class="focus"> with rx="3", plus .t: 1 to 4 words, sentence case, the actor or stage, no punctuation and no verbs in title case; and .d: the concrete mechanism, with real flags, endpoints, file names, sizes, no marketing, no restatement of the title, no trailing period. A 70-high card takes two description lines, a 90/100-high side card three. focus marks the component that does the work or coordinates the branch; one per diagram, with a 2 px accent border against 1.2 for a card.

88. Branch and loop patterns

Side input, dashed into the main column: <path class="dash" d="M 250 173 H 290" marker-end="url(#flowHead)"/>

Side output: <path class="dash" d="M 770 399 H 796" marker-end="url(#flowHead)"/>

A side input stops 10 px before the main card; a side output stops 4 px before the side box. A side stub uses mid at top + h/2 on 70-, 90- and 100-high boxes.

Exit branch with no return, such as a missing prerequisite: <path class="dash" d="M 300 173 H 254" marker-end="url(#flowHead)"/>

Fallback that rejoins a lower card: <path class="dash" d="M 910 454 V 605 H 780" marker-end="url(#flowHead)"/> with its .side label.

Loopback, a later card feeding an earlier one. Four lanes per side, 24 px inside the side box edge and 20 apart: left 226, 206, 186, 166; right 824, 844, 864, 884. The spine runs under the side box band and is written before the first card rect.

<path class="loop" d="M 300 266 H 226 V 80 H 300" marker-end="url(#flowHead)"/>

That is card 3 (top 246) back to card 1 (top 30): out at hi 266, up lane 1, in at lo 80. The spine covers the rows between the cards and disappears under a side box that sits on them.

  • Dashed is optional, alternate or side; dotted is a return; solid is the main path. A loopback runs upward only; a forward jump stays dash.
  • Route around cards, never across them. Only a loop spine passes under a side box, by paint order. Labels stay 10 px from a card border.
  • Lane 1 carries the shortest span, lane 2 the next; spans nest or stay disjoint.
  • A loopback joins main cards: out of the lower card at hi, into the upper at lo; mid belongs to a side stub. Two connectors on one edge take different marks.
  • The joined rows must be free of a side box on that side, and a spine passes at most one box.
  • One loopback per lane, at most four per side; more than two per diagram reads better as two sections.
  • An optional label names the returning payload in up to 12 characters, in the row gap nearest the arrowhead, 6 px from the spine, never over a card.

Two arrows leave the parent bottom at the card centres, 411 and 659, and enter the children at the same x. No horizontal fork line, no elbows. A merge back into a 470 card keeps the same pair, two arrows at 411 and 659 with captions at 425 and 673 where they add something. When one branch ends and only one continues, the gap carries one arrow at x=535 and one caption at x=549.

  • The single column stays the default. A split needs a decision with two named outcomes, and the decision card is the focus.
  • Both children leave the same row and are normal cards with a title and up to two description lines.
  • Both take 28 characters, the 186 px width of a 220 side card, with text at 318 and 566.
  • Captions sit at 425 and 673 and take 18 characters, so each stays inside x=790.
  • The inner edges at 522 and 548 carry no connector. A loop attaches at the outer edge, x=300 or x=770, at a free mark.
  • A side box may sit on a split row, one per side: the left box feeds the left card, the right box the right card.
  • Split arrows stay muted, and take the accent only when the focus card is the decision and one branch is the main action.

99. Section copy

  • h2 pattern: subject: outcome, lowercase, as in video download: url to file.
  • Note: the rule that drives the diagram, not a walkthrough.
  • Tag: the invariant or verdict shape in one line, as in one run ends in one verdict: finished, failed rc, or stopped by escape.
  • Footer: what the paths share, or the one guarantee that holds across them.
  • The copy follows the writing rules in SKILL.md; keys and literal input render in straight single quotes: 's', '0', 'ready'.

1111. Validation

The page is right when it follows the numbers in sections 5 to 8 and keeps template.html unchanged: head script, token block, toolbar, toolbar script and print rules. Check the written part by reading it once, by eye: geometry, arrows and captions, text baselines and budgets, the tag and viewBox height, the focus and accents, the loop lanes and the marker ids. Do not count, grep, diff or hash the page with commands, and do not compare it against the template; a check is a read, not a measurement. A diagram added to a page that already holds one needs only its own section checked. A defect found in the read gets one repair edit, and a defect that survives that edit is named in the reply to the user rather than chased in another pass.

The template, the toolbar, the themes and the print view are fixed and checked once in the repository. Do not re-check them per page, take screenshots or make PDFs. Open the finished page once for the user; the user's eye is the last check.

1313. Toolbar and modes

demo.html is the reference implementation. Copy its toolbar, token blocks and scripts; do not invent a second control style.

  • Fixed, 24 px high, 28 px inset, fill var(--page-bg), no bottom divider; the back button sits at the left end and the other controls stay right aligned. Below 680 px the bar wraps to two rows, the controls first and the two sliders second; the print and back labels and the dividers drop away and main top padding rises to 66 px.
  • Back button: at the left end of the bar, thin left arrow 1 px below centre plus lowercase go back at weight 300, 18 px high. The Navigation API decides whether an earlier entry exists and the button is disabled at opacity 0.4 when it does not. On a file: page that the API reports as the first entry, a ?via=1 marker on the arrival link and a history.length above 1 prove the Back entry, which covers Firefox local files where each file is its own origin. Browsers without the API fall back to history.length. It rechecks when the page is shown.
  • Print toggle: auto width, 18 px high, printer icon plus print friendly, aria-pressed; active fill is the toolbar ink.
  • Mode switch: 44 x 20 px, moon left, sun right, 16 px thumb, aria-checked true in light.
  • Theme buttons: text plus a solid caret, 18 px high, 68 px minimum width, a menu below each.
  • Brightness and saturation sliders follow the two theme buttons: an icon, a 48 px track with a 10 px thumb, and the step readout. Seven steps from -3 to +3; 0 is the shipped palette. Each step scales brightness by 8 percent and saturation by 15 percent, applied to the six theme values after the theme pick.
  • The sliders change the applied palette only. The saved pick, the print palette and the link state are untouched, and a reload starts both at 0.
  • A menu lists its mode's names in role="menu" rows with menuitemradio and the current name marked aria-checked. Picking one applies it and switches mode.
  • Native <button> controls, so Enter and Space work. Opening a menu focuses its checked row; arrows move, Home and End jump, Escape closes the menu and returns focus to its button, a click outside closes it.
  • html[data-theme="light"|"dark"] selects the mode and its list. Each mode remembers its own theme; the switch flips between the two picks.
  • html[data-print="on"] forces the print tokens on a white page and leaves data-theme alone; off restores the previous mode.
  • Initial mode comes from prefers-color-scheme, followed until the mode switch or a theme pick. No storage.
  • Tokens live on :root; the theme script writes the six adjusted values and --pick-ink on every change, and print aliases its fixed values there too, so it wins without a cascade trick.
  • One inline head script sets the initial data-theme before paint; one script before </body> holds the theme table, builds the menus, applies the theme and follows OS changes.
  • A link to a local .html page carries the reader's state. The toolbar script writes ?mode=...&dark=...&light=...&via=1 into every local page link on load and after each pick, and reads those values on load. The query carries the mode and two names, never the six values; each page resolves them through its own theme table, so pages from different projects can keep the same names with different palettes. A page opened from an index therefore keeps the mode and both theme choices. The via=1 marker records that the page arrived through a generated link and is what the back button reads on a local file in Firefox. A user change rewrites the current history entry with the same query and keeps the marker through history.replaceState, so browser Back and a reload keep the pick. No storage; a link with no query opens on the OS mode and the default choices.

1414. Index pages

A set of finished diagram pages and documents may open on one index page. It is a separate page type: copy index.html instead of template.html. Sections 1 to 13 keep their rules for the diagram pages the index links; this chapter covers the index itself.

Order: toolbar, h1, .sub, one or more page sections, zero or more document sections, then an empty footer that draws the closing rule. The subtitle is one sentence at 15 px on the muted ink, larger than the diagram page subtitle; it names the set and its reach and is the only intro. No second note sits under it.

A section opens with a .head, an h2 pill naming its group in lowercase, as in sample pages or setup, then its cards or its table. The pill is the only label. Two to six entries per page section.

An entry is one link card:

<a class="entry" href="page-one.html">
  <svg class="glyph" viewBox="0 0 104 56" aria-hidden="true" focusable="false">
    <rect x="2" y="20" width="24" height="16" rx="2"/>
    <rect x="40" y="20" width="24" height="16" rx="2"/>
    <rect x="78" y="20" width="24" height="16" rx="2"/>
    <path class="hot" d="M 6 20 V 10 H 98 V 20 M 52 10 V 20"/>
  </svg>
  <span class="name">Page one</span>
</a>
  • href is the page file name. The toolbar script appends the theme query to every local .html link; do not write a query by hand.
  • Name: 1 to 7 words, sentence case, up to 44 characters. No description: the glyph and the name carry the page.
  • Glyph: 104 x 56, shapes between x 2 and 102 and y 4 and 52. Rects and paths only, no text. Muted stroke 1.4; zero or one .hot path in accent at 1.6. A few objects that suggest the page, not a legend.
  • Documents: one section per group, the h2 pill and a .docs table with Document and Covers columns, one row per file. The file name links the file. The table is laid out fixed with the first column at 32 percent, so the Document and Covers columns line up across every section on the page whatever the text is; a long name wraps inside its column instead of widening it.
  • Split documents by subject once the list passes about six rows or covers more than one subject, as in user manuals, setup, operation and reference. Two to eight rows per table; a group below two rows joins its neighbour.
  • The copy follows the writing rules in SKILL.md: plain and dry, no owner or user references, no slogans, "X, not Y" contrasts or superlatives, no manual line breaks, ASCII only. A group name and a document name state their subject.
  • The entries, glyphs and names are checked by reading against the bounds above. The template is fixed and checked in the repository; do not re-test the toolbar, both modes or print on the index. Open the index once for the user.
  • Limits: an index lists pages; it does not explain them. No counts, badges, status rows or diagrams inside a glyph.

Embedding a page

A finished diagram page can sit inside another HTML page, toolbar and theme included, as one iframe with no second copy. Place the line at the end of the host content, just before </main>:

<iframe src="sample-one-diagram.html" title="DNS lookup flow" style="width:100%;height:1020px;border:1px solid #9a9a9a;margin-top:18px"></iframe>
  • The embedded page sits in the host's folder or below it, because a page opened from file:// frames only its own tree. title names it for a screen reader.
  • Height comes from the viewBox, not from the eye: 330 + (iframe width - 42) * H / 1040, rounded up to the next 20, where H is the viewBox height. The 330 is the page chrome outside the svg: main padding, title, subtitle, section head and note, with room for an optional footer. An 808-high diagram in a 926 px column lands on 1020.
  • The box keeps the page's own toolbar and theme. The host's mode is not pushed into it, and the toolbar scrolls with the box, not the window.
  • A host rebuilt from Markdown loses the line, so it is added again after conversion. No print variant and no second copy.
